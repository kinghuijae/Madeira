# Madeira

## Logs

When the user mentions a log or a report ("로그", "로그 봐줘", "방금 로그 올렸어",
"the latest log") without naming another source, they mean the game report sent
from the app with **Send Logs to GitHub**. Use the `game-triage` skill: fetch it
with `python tools/game-report.py fetch` and triage from there. Do not ask which
log they mean or look for local log files first.
